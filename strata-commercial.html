How Our Work Is Delivered

  1. 1
    On-site Assessment & Reporting We inspect, photograph, and document all problem areas. Scope is clarified visually and a detailed proposal is sent to council/contact.
  2. 2
    Quote & Scheduling We provide fixed-amount quotes (no hidden extras), clarify timelines, access needs and coordinate with building managers for seamless execution.
  3. 3
    Work Execution & Site Management All work is carried out cleanly, quietly, and safely. Parkade and access control, safety barricades, and post-work cleanup handled by our team.
  4. 4
    Results & Close-out Review All work is documented with final photos, council/manager walk-through and warranty certificate issued upon completion.
